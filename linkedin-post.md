# LinkedIn post draft

I turned my weekly grocery workflow into a reusable agent skill.

It keeps a persistent grocery list, checks what is due for the week, looks for current deals and coupons, compares delivered costs, and proposes substitutions when they actually make sense.

The important part is the boundary: it prepares an approval queue, but it does not place an order or touch payment information.

I packaged the workflow with ChatGPT-style and Claude-style instructions, fictional sample data, configuration examples, privacy rules, and validation tests.
